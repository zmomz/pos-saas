# ISSUE-066 — Retail modifiers/combos in catalog

**Status:** ready-for-agent · **Type:** AFK · **Sprint:** 4 · **Findings:** P2 catalog · **Stories:** 52

## Parent
[PRD — Platform Remediation](../prd/PRD-AUDIT-platform-remediation.md)

## What to build
Modifiers/combos exist in the restaurant vertical; surface them in the retail catalog so a retail merchant can attach modifier groups and build combos for non-restaurant business types.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] A retail merchant can attach modifier groups and define combos from the retail catalog UI.
- [ ] Retail combos deduct component stock at sale (consistent with ISSUE-032).
- [ ] Covered by a test.

## Blocked by
- ISSUE-032, ISSUE-040
