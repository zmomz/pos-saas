# ISSUE-075 — Reprint past receipts

**Status:** ready-for-agent · **Type:** AFK · **Sprint:** 4 · **Findings:** P2 POS · **Stories:** 54

## Parent
[PRD — Platform Remediation](../prd/PRD-AUDIT-platform-remediation.md)

## What to build
Add the ability to look up a past sale and reprint its receipt (re-rendering from stored sale data via the renderer), in Arabic, honoring the template.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] A cashier can find a past sale and reprint its receipt.
- [ ] The reprint matches the original (template, Arabic, totals, QR).
- [ ] Covered by a test of the reprint payload.
